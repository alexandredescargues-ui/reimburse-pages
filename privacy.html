Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

About REIMBURSE

REIMBURSE is a personal macOS automation application used to organize invoices and expense records.

Information processed

The application processes invoice documents, billing dates, invoice identifiers, amounts, and related expense information. Invoice documents may contain personal or business information.

Google API access

REIMBURSE accesses Google Drive and Google Sheets with the account holder's authorization to store invoice documents, check existing records, and manage expense information.

Storage and sharing

The application operates locally on the operator's computer. Invoice PDFs are stored in an authorized Google Drive folder, which may be shared with the operator's employer. Temporary local files may be created during processing.

Data use

Google user data is used for the invoice and expense management functions described above. REIMBURSE does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Security

Google authorization credentials are stored locally. Access to Google services is governed by the permissions granted to the application and the access controls of the relevant Google account.

Data retention and deletion

Invoice documents and expense records remain in the authorized Google services until removed by an authorized user. Local temporary files are managed by the application. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
